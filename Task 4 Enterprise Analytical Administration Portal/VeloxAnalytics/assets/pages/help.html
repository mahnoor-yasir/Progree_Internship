Frequently Asked Questions

Click to expand
How do I generate a report? Click to expand
Navigate to the Reports section, select a report template (Sales, Performance, Financial, or Customers), configure your parameters, and click Generate. The report will be available for download in PDF or CSV format.
How do I switch between dark and light mode? Click to expand
Click the theme toggle button in the sidebar (moon/sun icon) or go to Settings > Theme Preferences to switch between dark and light mode. Your preference is saved automatically in your browser.
Can I export data to Excel/CSV? Click to expand
Yes! Use the Export button in the Data Manager section. You can export to CSV or JSON formats. Reports can be exported as PDF or CSV.
How do I add new records? Click to expand
Go to the Data Manager section and click the "Add Record" button. Fill in the required fields (Customer, Product, Category, Amount) and click "Save Record" to add a new entry.
How do I create a new user account? Click to expand
Go to Settings > User Management and click "Add User". Enter the user's name, email, role (Admin, Manager, Analyst, Viewer), and set a password. Click "Create User" to add the new account.
How do I filter data in Data Manager? Click to expand
Use the Search bar to find specific records, use the Category dropdown to filter by product category, or use the Status dropdown to filter by order status. Click column headers to sort data.
Is my data secure? Click to expand
Yes! Velox Analytics uses industry-standard security practices. All data is encrypted and stored locally in your browser's secure storage. Session data is protected with secure tokens.
What are the keyboard shortcuts? Click to expand
Ctrl + K - Search
Esc - Close modal
Ctrl + R - Refresh data
Ctrl + N - New record
How do I update my profile? Click to expand
Click on your profile avatar in the top-right corner and select "My Profile". You can update your name, email, and change your password from there.
How do I delete multiple records at once? Click to expand
In Data Manager, check the checkbox next to each record you want to delete, or click the "Select All" checkbox at the top. Then click the "Delete Selected" button and confirm the deletion.
How do I view charts in Analytics? Click to expand
Go to the Analytics section. You'll see three charts: Revenue Trends (line chart), Sales by Category (doughnut chart), and Regional Performance (bar chart). Use the filters to customize your view.
How do I download a chart as an image? Click to expand
Each chart has a download button (↓ icon) in the top-right corner. Click it to download the chart as a PNG image. This works for all charts in Dashboard and Analytics.
How do I import data? Click to expand
In Data Manager, click the "Import" button. Select a JSON or CSV file from your computer. The data will be imported and added to your existing records.
What is the difference between Dashboard and Analytics? Click to expand
Dashboard provides a high-level overview with KPI cards and summary charts. Analytics offers more detailed charts with advanced filtering options for deeper data analysis.
